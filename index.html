Filipino Pork Steak (Pork Bistek / Bistek na Baboy)

Ingredients

  • Meat & Marinade
  • 800g–1kg pork chops or pork shoulder (kasim), sliced ¼-inch thick
  • 4–6 tbsp soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup calamansi juice (or lime/lemon juice)
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp sugar (to balance flavor)
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce (optional, richer taste)
  • ½ cup water
For Cooking
  • 3 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2–3 large onions, sliced into rings
  • Extra salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinade: Combine pork, soy sauce, calamansi juice, garlic, pepper, sugar, and oyster sauce (if using). Mix well and chill for at least 30 minutes (1–2 hours is better); reserve marinade.
  2. Heat oil over medium heat. Pan-fry pork 2–3 mins per side until golden brown. Set aside.
  3. Sauté onions: In the same pan, cook the onion rings briefly until fragrant. Set aside.
  4. Simmer: Put pork back in. Pour reserved marinade + water. Bring to boil, then lower heat, cover, and simmer 25–40 mins until tender and sauce reduces.
  5. Finish: Add remaining onion rings, simmer 1–2 mins more. Adjust salt/sugar if needed.
  6. Serve: Best with hot steamed rice!
